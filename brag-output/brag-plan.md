# /brag — Mantis AI launch video

## Angle
Mantis is positioned as **intent-based lead intelligence**, not another lead list. The video has to
land the deck's core line — *a lead tells you who, signals tell you why* — using the real product,
not slides. Every frame is the actual Mantis dashboard running locally against the real leads
database (251,077 businesses), captured live.

## Hook
The dark Mantis map, dense with pins, and one question: **"Which businesses are worth calling?"**
The answer is already on screen — a real opportunity card with a 59% score and TOP SIGNALS
(No Website, Low Rating).

## Highlights
1. **Signals, not listings** — the map opportunity card: score gauge + signal chips.
2. **Just ask** — typing `Clinics in Gurgaon that don't have a website` into the real composer,
   and Mantis returning *"Found 25 medical clinic near Gurgaon without a website"* as a scored,
   selectable table (rating, area, website status, opportunity score).
3. **From signal to contact** — selecting rows, "Add to leads", and the Leads screen with real
   business names, addresses, contacts and an Export button.

## Punchline
Mantis wordmark + **"Find businesses ready to buy."** + mantisai.in

## Tone
`polished` — serious, restrained, product-led. No jokes, no fake numbers. Everything on screen is
real output from the running app.

## Visual identity (taken from the product's own tokens, app/globals.css)
- Brand green `#a8d51e`, dark green `#648b1c`, ink `#101214`, cream `#fcfcfa`, border `#e5e8e2`
- Amber signal `#f5a623` (the "no website" pins and chips)
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (product UI font), Fraunces for the product's own display headings
- Radii and shadows mirrored from `--radius-lg` / `--shadow-card`

## Edit notes
Cuts are hard, each with a ~0.17s dip through the product's own cream (`--g-cream`). A plain
crossfade between the dark map and the light dashboard produced a muddy double exposure, so no
dissolve survived into the final cut.

## Notes
- Contact numbers of real businesses are blurred in scene 6 — the column stays visible, the digits
  do not, because the video is public and those are live small-business numbers.
- Everything else is unretouched product output.
